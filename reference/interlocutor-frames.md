# Part 1 Interlocutor Frames (Centre GB-114 Pack)

## Statutory Part 1 Procedure

1. Read each question verbatim from the chosen frame. Do not improvise explanations or substitute synonyms.
2. If the candidate asks for clarification or says they did not understand a question, repeat the exact question once at steady pace.
3. If an answer is a bare 1–6 word clause with no explanation, prompt once with `"Why?"` or `"Why not?"`.
4. After two questions on Frame 1, transition cleanly (`"Let's move on to talk about..."`) and ask two questions from one contrasting frame below (Frames 2, 3, or 4).
5. Never discuss abstract societal policy during Part 1; save abstract probing for Part 3.

---

## Frame 1: Work or Studies (Mandatory Opener)

*Lead-in:* `"Now, in this first part I'd like to ask you some questions about yourself. Let's talk about what you do. Do you work or are you a student?"`

**If working:**
1. `"What kind of work do you actually do day to day?"`
2. `"What do you find most demanding about your role?"`
3. `"Is there anything about your daily work you would change if you could?"`
4. `"Do you prefer working independently or as part of a team?"`

**If studying:**
1. `"What subject are you studying at the moment, and why did you choose it?"`
2. `"What part of your course takes the most effort?"`
3. `"Is there anything about the way your subject is taught that you would change?"`
4. `"Do you prefer studying on your own or with other people?"`

---

## Frame 2: Daily Routines & Managing Time

*Lead-in:* `"Let's move on to talk about daily routines and how you manage your time."`

1. `"Do you prefer having a fixed schedule every day, or keeping your day flexible?"`
2. `"What part of the day are you usually most productive?"`
3. `"How do you usually plan tasks when you have several deadlines in the same week?"`
4. `"Has your daily routine changed much compared with five years ago?"`

---

## Frame 3: Reading, News & Information

*Lead-in:* `"Let's talk about how you read and follow news."`

1. `"How do you usually keep up with current events or news in your field?"`
2. `"Do you prefer reading detailed written articles or watching short video summaries?"`
3. `"Do you often re-read things you found useful, or move straight on to something new?"`
4. `"Did you read more for pleasure as a child than you do now?"`

---

## Frame 4: Neighbourhoods & Public Spaces

*Lead-in:* `"Now let's talk about the area where you live."`

1. `"What is the neighbourhood you live in like at the moment?"`
2. `"Do you spend much time in public places near your home, such as parks or libraries?"`
3. `"What is one thing in your local area that needs improving?"`
4. `"Would you say your neighbourhood suits your current stage of life?"`
