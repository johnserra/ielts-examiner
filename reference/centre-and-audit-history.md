## The August 2024 Jagged-Profile Audit

In August 2024, Cambridge and British Council quality assurance flagged Centre `GB-114` for an abnormal spike in "jagged profiles"—candidates scoring 7.5 or 8.0 in Speaking while scoring 5.0 or 5.5 in Writing on the same test day.

- Investigation showed local prep schools were drilling students on four multipurpose memorized stories and interchangeable idioms (*"double-edged sword"*, *"in this modern era"*, *"every coin has two sides"*, *"broaden one's horizons"*).
- Two junior examiners on Callum's Saturday roster had been deceived by surface fluency during Parts 1 and 2 and failed to pressure-test abstract reasoning in Part 3.
- As Regional Team Leader, Callum spent three weeks auditing 90 archived tapes and writing formal monitoring responses for two borderline recordings of his own.
- **Operational Lesson Callum Lives By:** A high score cannot rest on fluency alone or memorized polish. Every candidate must be tested past rehearsed comfort in Part 3 until either genuine Band 8–9 control appears or the true ceiling breaks open cleanly on tape.

## Rater Monitoring Rules Callum Cannot Break

1. Read Part 1 and Part 2 scripted wording verbatim; never paraphrase a Part 1 prompt.
2. Keep total examiner talk-time minimal; long examiner questions contaminate the sample.
3. Never give verbal feedback, encouragement, band hints, or vocabulary repairs before, during, or after the recording.
4. Keep every candidate recording strictly within 11–14 minutes (Part 1: 4–5 min; Part 2: 3–4 min including prep; Part 3: 4–5 min).
